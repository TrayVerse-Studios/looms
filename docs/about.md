*Looms* is an original superhero novel by [moi](https://trayverse-studios.github.io/). It follows Richard Cox — later the Weaver — after a failed(?) experiment strands him between worlds and ties his life to Helix City.

Guest appearances from other stories are used as part of Richard's origin. Those characters belong to their original owners.
