A short guide to the Helix City roster. Details come from the novel and the Looms lore notes.

<div class="card-grid">
  <div class="card">
    <h2>Richard Cox</h2>
    <p class="alias">The Weaver</p>
    <p>Game developer from Helix City who gains probability powers after Clara Vance's dark-matter machine explodes. His suit carries a tungsten W and an AI named F.R.I.E.N.D.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="card">
    <h2>Trevor Matondo</h2>
    <p class="alias">Shadowbolt</p>
    <p>A speedster from Earth-404 who meets Richard inside the Speed Force. Thinker-level mind, black-and-gold lightning, and a pocket dimension lab.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="card">
    <h2>Eliza Nakamura</h2>
    <p class="alias">Kangae</p>
    <p>Richard's neighbour and Helix City's first public post-dark-matter hero. Telepathy and telekinesis. The press called her Cerebra; she prefers Kangae.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="card">
    <h2>Grace Cox</h2>
    <p class="alias">Overwatch</p>
    <p>Richard's younger sister and mission control. Lives with a serious illness; later events complicate that fight with a second state called Trajectory.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="card">
    <h2>Priya Okafor</h2>
    <p class="alias">Arachne</p>
    <p>Student hero who generates organic spider silk. Grounds the younger team and travels with Trevor's group.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="card">
    <h2>Damian Cross</h2>
    <p class="alias">Verdict</p>
    <p>Ashfield lawyer by day, harsh vigilante by night. Operates in The Hollows and argues that some people already had their chance.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="card">
    <h2>Clara Vance</h2>
    <p class="alias">Antagonist</p>
    <p>Former executive who forced Richard to build a multiverse machine, then hunted him across worlds in powered armour.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="card">
    <h2>Rowan Ellery</h2>
    <p class="alias">The diner</p>
    <p>Owner of Ellery Diner. The novel opens with Richard crashing through his roof and telling the origin story over the wreckage.</p>
  </div>
</div>
