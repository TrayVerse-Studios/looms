Darkness. That’s all it was. Deep, deep, dark. Cerebra looked around the endless void she now stood in. Blackness reigned in every direction. There was nothing. Then, she saw something — a light. A faint ember glow dwelled on the ground at her feet. It pulsed brightly as it began to sprout. Cerebra stepped back as roots emerged from the light. They spread far out, then grew similar growths on each tip, forming their own set of roots. Intertwining vines dispersed, their luminescence waging war against the bleak abyss, as the central shine matured into a Great Basin bristlecone pine. Its trunk grew wide and uniquely warped, creating a stable foundation for countless branches that spread to support leaves that gleamed as brightly as its origin.
